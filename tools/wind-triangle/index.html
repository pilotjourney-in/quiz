How it is calculated

Formulae used

  • sin(WCA) = (wind speed ÷ TAS) × sin(wind direction − true course).
  • True heading = true course + WCA. A positive WCA is a right correction.
  • Groundspeed = TAS × cos(WCA) − wind speed × cos(wind direction − true course).
  • Magnetic heading = true heading − easterly variation + westerly variation.
  • Time = distance ÷ groundspeed. Fuel = time × fuel flow.

For training/study only, not for real flight planning. Variation in northern India is typically about 1° E.

How the wind triangle is solved

The wind triangle is the same problem you solve on the wind face of an E6B flight computer. You know the true airspeed and the true course you want to make good over the ground. The wind is given as the direction it is coming from and its speed. The missing sides are the heading you must fly and the groundspeed that results. This page uses the standard sine formula rather than a mechanical computer, but the geometry is identical.

The sine of the wind correction angle is the wind speed divided by true airspeed, multiplied by the sine of the angle between the wind and the true course. A positive correction means the wind is from the right and you steer to the right of the course; a negative correction means you steer left. True heading is the true course plus that correction. Groundspeed is true airspeed times the cosine of the correction, minus the tail or head component of the wind along the course. If the wind is stronger than the airspeed at a right angle, no heading can hold the course, and the calculator flags that case.

Magnetic heading follows the exam phrase east is least, west is best: subtract an easterly variation and add a westerly one. If you add a distance, time is distance divided by groundspeed. Fuel used is that time multiplied by fuel flow. The worked example is a westbound leg from the Delhi area at 120 knots, with a north-westerly wind and a one degree easterly variation typical of northern India. Treat every result as study practice, not as a flight-planning output.

Sample questions

  1. Two aeroplanes are converging at about the same altitude. Aeroplane X sees aeroplane Y approaching from its right. What should happen?
  2. Two aircraft are approaching each other head-on with a risk of collision. What is each pilot required to do?
  3. An aircraft is treated as overtaking when it approaches another from behind along a line making an angle of less than how many degrees with the other aircraft's plane of symmetry?
  4. A light aeroplane and a glider are converging at about the same level. Which must give way?
  5. A glider and a free balloon are converging at about the same level. Which gives way?
